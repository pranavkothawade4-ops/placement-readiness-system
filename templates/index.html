The 5 Core Pillars of Placement Readiness

Placements are never just about CGPA or coding alone. Our proprietary Placement Readiness Index (PRI) measures the exact criteria recruiters evaluate during campus hiring drives.

1. Academic Foundation

15 Pts

Evaluates CGPA cutoffs, academic consistency across semesters, and active backlogs that determine eligibility for Tier-1 and Day-1 placement drives.

2. Technical & DSA Mastery

35 Pts

Assesses algorithmic depth, LeetCode / HackerRank problem counts, core programming languages, and Computer Science fundamentals (OS, DBMS, CN).

3. Practical Projects & Experience

20 Pts

Measures full-stack applications, real-world internships, hackathons, open-source GitHub contributions, and cloud-deployed live URLs.

4. Aptitude & Reasoning Speed

15 Pts

Quant, logical puzzles, and analytical reasoning required to clear the preliminary online screening rounds (Round 1) where 70% of applicants are filtered out.

5. Soft Skills & Interview Articulation

15 Pts

Verbal fluency, technical communication, confidence, leadership experiences, and proficiency with the STAR behavioral interview framework.
